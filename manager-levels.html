Description

Manager
You lead a team with regular support from your manager. You're developing your people management skills. You help your team succeed by providing clear direction, assigning appropriate projects, and providing feedback consistently. You coach your directs on their career progression, and build and maintain effective cross-functional relationships.

Senior Manager
You oversee the vision and execution of multiple major projects or business areas. You guide your team and influence other teams or departments to help achieve your team's goals. You lead initiatives that span multiple teams.

Director
You lead a department OR a function within a large department, and drive major initiatives. You forge strong relationships with cross-functional partners to remove roadblocks for your team, and have a vision for the team 1 to 2 years in the future. You exemplify the values of Figma and hold others accountable to do the same.

Senior Director
You generally lead multiple teams and/or departments. You drive innovative and impactful initiatives across different areas of the business. You are skilled at navigating the overall organization to support your team/department's efforts. You have a long term vision for your teams, and are able to rally others around that vision. You find scalable ways to impact Figma's culture across the company.

VP
You lead an organization within the company, overseeing multiple layers of managers. You create, inspire, lead and drive the organization's functional strategy towards Figma's long term vision. You guide priorities on your team and others, and collaborate with fellow leaders on the larger vision.

(Sr) VP
You oversee the operations, strategy, and the vision of an entire functional area of a company.
Scope

The team
You're developing your leadership craft (e.g., leadership trainings, working with mentors, etc)
When you're unsure you quickly get help from your manager.
You guide and support your team in effective execution of multiple projects.

The team
At this level, your scope and focus expands—perhaps through different types of team members, managing managers, overseeing a larger budget, or wider impact of projects.
You are an emerging thought-leader who proactively brings ideas and strategies to your manager for their feedback.
You're accountable for the success of your broader team's work.

The department
You are leading and building a department, and are accountable for the department's impact.
You're a proactive cross-functional partner and accountable for the performance of your department's work.
You are leading a strategic part of the business and guide multiple teams that are leading multiple cross functional projects.

The department
You generally only have people managers as your direct reports.
You are supporting and taking accountability for the company's most important initiatives.
You own and lead a larger strategic area of the business.

The organization
You direct multiple levels of management.
You shape all activities of the organization, and how they tie to the larger company strategy.
You and your team are core contributors to defining and achieving our company-wide objectives with visible, sustainable, and meaningful impact.

The company
You are a key member of the senior leadership team, and are a visible role model throughout the whole company.Along with other execs, you set and evolve the strategic direction of your org and the whole company.You set the tone for talent management and development for the org and beyond. All Execs function as “Chief Talent Officers.”
